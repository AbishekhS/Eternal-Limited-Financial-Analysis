## 📌 Project Overview
An institutional-grade 5-year financial model, ratio analysis, and segment unit economics study of **Eternal Limited** (formerly Zomato Limited), synthesized directly from 5 audited consolidated annual reports covering **FY2021-22 through FY2025-26** (>1,500 pages of statutory filings).

The model analyzes Eternal's financial transformation from an early cash-burning food delivery aggregator into a multi-engine, cash-generative consumer platform anchored by **Quick Commerce (Blinkit)** and **Food Delivery (Zomato)**.

> **Note on Methodology:** Statutory filing extraction, multi-year footnote cross-referencing (Ind AS 115 Note 34 and Ind AS 116 lease schedules), and balance sheet reconciliations across the 5 annual reports were supported using Google DeepMind's Antigravity agentic workspace.

## 🔍 Key Strategic & Financial Findings

### 1. The Inventory Accounting Paradigm Shift (FY26)
Reported revenue surged **+168.6% YoY** in FY26. Under **Ind AS 115**, Blinkit transitioned from booking commission take-rates to recording full gross merchandise value as revenue and wholesale merchandise procurement as COGS. While reported Gross Margin contracted mathematically from 72.5% to 44.5%, absolute gross profit expanded significantly, and operational efficiency improved.

### 2. The J-Curve Breakeven & ESOP Operating Leverage
Bottom-line inflection occurred in **FY24** (PAT swung +₹1,322 Cr into positive territory). The single biggest structural driver was **operating leverage from employee stock options**: ESOP costs collapsed from **20.9% of revenue in FY22 to just 1.5% in FY26**.

### 3. Quick Commerce Surpasses Food Delivery
In FY26, Quick Commerce Net Order Value (**₹48,567 Cr**) officially flipped past Food Delivery NOV (**₹37,993 Cr**). Having absorbed the upfront capex of scaling to 2,243 dark stores across 250+ cities, Blinkit achieved **Adjusted EBITDA breakeven in Q3 FY26**.

### 4. Negative Working Capital Advantage
Eternal operates on a negative Cash Conversion Cycle (**-4.8 days** in FY26). Instant B2C UPI/card settlements (**DSO of 11.8 days**) combined with FMCG supplier credit terms (**DPO of 36.4 days**) mean operations are self-financing.

## 🗂️ Workbook Architecture (`Eternals Company Analysis.xlsx`)

The financial model consists of **8 integrated sheets**:

1. **`Dashboard`**: High-level command center with 8 KPI cards, 6 spacious native Excel charts (no legend clutter, clear data labels), analytical briefs under every graph, and an Executive Master Data Table.
2. **`Company Overview`**: Corporate profile, platform pillars (Zomato, Blinkit, Hyperpure, District), organizational flowchart, and 2008–FY26 milestone timeline.
3. **`Financial Data`**: 5-year Consolidated P&L, Balance Sheet (`Assets - Equity - Liab = 0.0` audited check), Cash Flow Statement, and Memo items.
4. **`Ratio Analysis`**: 19 formula-driven ratios grouped into Profitability, Liquidity, Solvency/Leverage, and Working Capital/Efficiency with analytical commentary.
5. **`Growth Analysis`**: YoY growth rates, 4-year CAGRs, and operating leverage breakdown.
6. **`Segment Analysis`**: Deep dive into external revenues, Adjusted EBITDA, and operational KPIs (NOV, MTUs, Dark Stores) across all 4 platforms.
7. **`Conclusion`**: Institutional SWOT analysis, investment thesis, and FY27–FY30 management guidance roadmap.
8. **`Source Register`**: 17-item audit registry linking every financial figure to its exact annual report PDF filename, report section, and page number.
